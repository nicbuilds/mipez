07 / PERMITS AND VALIDATION

Regulatory items and missing inputs

Fees and permits

  • Commercial aquaculture concession/permit: confirm the applicable modality and current fee.
  • Environmental assessment and applicable SEMARNAT authorizations.
  • Federal-zone concession/occupation, if applicable.
  • ZOFEMAT works, maritime signaling and navigation permits, as applicable to the design.
  • Health, organism-movement and traceability requirements.

Required validations

  • Coordinates, bathymetry, waves, currents, substrate and carrying capacity.
  • Legal source, price and annual availability of 12,500 juveniles.
  • Survival, implantation yield and saleable percentage by batch.
  • Purchase contract or letters of intent for 9,375 mabés/year at MXN $450.
  • Three quotations for infrastructure, vessel services, staff and laboratory.
  • 48-month cash-flow model, taxes, financing and contingencies.
Conclusion: 50,000 es el tamaño objetivo del escenario económico, no una recomendación para sembrar de inmediato. The project should proceed only si el estudio de sitio, la autoridad y el abastecimiento legal permiten esa escala y si se demuestra la demanda al precio de $450 MXN.
