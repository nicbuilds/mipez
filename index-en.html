02 / PRODUCTION MODEL

A modular marine
and land-based operation.

Integration of suspended longline culture with land-based quarantine, implantation, and controlled Recirculating Aquaculture Systems (RAS) to maximize nacre deposition.

Proposed species and system

Pteria sterna (rainbow-lip pearl oyster) and Pinctada imbricata, backed by scientific records of rapid nacre coating (8 to 12 months).

  • Marine validation phase: suspended longline system with lantern/pocket nets and a rented or shared light land-based laboratory near the port.
  • Intensive land-based phase (RAS): 1 mm HDPE geomembrane square tank (3×3×1 m, ~7,500 L operating volume) housing 4 cubic 1×1×1 m towers.
  • Biological yield ("Effective 2.0 Rule"): 2 to 3 hemispherical nuclei adhered per shell yield an average of 1.8 to 2.2 marketable mabé pearls per stocked oyster per year.
Applied Biology

03 / RAS HYDRODYNAMIC ENGINEERING

3×3 m Tank, Cubic Towers
& Gyre Flow (Option A).

High-density architecture housing 4,000 oysters (1,000 per 1×1×1 m module), ensuring laminar cross-flow of 3 to 8 cm/s across all 5 levels and continuous pseudofeces removal.

ARCH. 01 /

1×1×1 m Cubic Module with 5 Levels & Coroplast Deflectors

Built from 1" Schedule 40 PVC with 5 pull-out Egg Crate trays (95×95 cm). Each tray holds 10 rows of 20 oysters angled at 45° (comb-style layout = 200 oysters per level × 5 levels = 1,000 oysters per module). Between each level, a Coroplast sheet angled at 10°–15° deflects feces and pseudofeces into the central aisle without fouling the oysters below.

Step-by-Step Implementation of Option A (Gyre Pumps)

  • Step 1 · Sizing: 4 sine-wave cross-flow generators (Jebao SCP-180 / MCP-180 rated at 18,000–24,000 L/h, 75W) + UPS battery backup.
  • Step 2 · Vertical Mounting: Vertical attachment with heavy-duty dual cable ties on the front 1" PVC post at exactly 50 cm depth.
  • Step 3 · Carousel Effect: Clockwise orientation of all 4 modules in the corners of the 3×3 m tank with directional cages angled 5°–10° inward.
  • Step 4 · Electrical Protection: Synchronized controllers (Master/Slave) housed inside an IP66 waterproof enclosure.
  • Step 5 · Wave/Pulse Programming: 30% baseline power and 75%–80% pulse bursts (4 s pulse / 2 s rest) to stimulate gill pumping and clear detritus.
  • Step 6 · Tracer Calibration: Tray-crossing time of 12 to 25 seconds across the 1-meter rack (target velocity of 3 to 8 cm/s).

04 / SITE ASSESSMENT & REGULATION

La Paz Bay & aquaculture
regulatory framework.

Technical selection of marine polygons, oceanographic monitoring, and strict compliance with CONAPESCA, SEMARNAT, and CONANP prior to installation.

Site selection & critical variables

  • 1. Pre-select three polygons: review marine spatial planning, uses, access, navigation, and exclusion of sensitive areas (Espíritu Santo Archipelago National Park).
  • 2. Two stations per polygon: bathymetry, substrate type, currents, wave exposure, and storm protection.
  • 3. 8–12 week campaign + 12-month monitoring: temperature, salinity, dissolved oxygen, pH, turbidity, chlorophyll-a, and absence of pollutant discharges.
NOM-058-SAG/PESC/SEMARNAT-2013

Regulatory pathway & permits

  • Commercial Aquaculture Permit (CONAPESCA): up to 60 business days resolution with complete technical dossier (DOF Agreement Aug 26, 2025). Contact: administracion.acuicola@conapesca.gob.mx · Tel. 800 667 4022 (ext. 58572 / 58562).
  • Environmental Impact Assessment (SEMARNAT): Environmental Impact Statement (MIA) or preventive report depending on works and location.
  • Complementary clearances: federal maritime zone concession, maritime signaling, and aquatic health transport permits.

A case demanding
rigor.

MÉRIDA, YUCATÁN · SEP 14, 2026 / LA PAZ, BCS · OCT 31, 2026

The Government of Yucatán confirmed the detection of Naegleria fowleri in an artificial lake following a fatal case associated with the site. The official release reported the closure of the lake and an investigation into its maintenance. [1]

Infection is extremely rare and occurs when contaminated water enters through the nose. It is not contracted by drinking water. [1] The CDC emphasizes proper maintenance and disinfection—principles that govern both recreational water safety and biosecurity in Recirculating Aquaculture Systems (RAS). [2]
