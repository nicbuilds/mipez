01 / MIPEZ MISSION

An extraordinary marine animal. An opportunity to research a better way to grow.

MIPEZ aims to move existing scientific findings into the next stage: a carefully monitored pilot to test juvenile grow-out in real coastal conditions, with biosecurity, traceability and environmental assessment built in.

01 / PURPOSE

A resource under pressure

Sea cucumber extraction has raised concerns about the condition of wild populations. Responsible aquaculture may complement—not immediately replace—fisheries management and conservation.

02 / PURPOSE

Science before scale

We build on research conducted in Ría Lagartos with Holothuria floridana. The next step is to validate repeatability, survival, growth and seasonal management before considering expansion.

03 / PURPOSE

Traceability from day one

The project prioritizes legally sourced and authorized animals, batch records, movement controls and permits. No unauthorized juvenile collection or release into the wild is proposed.

02 / RESEARCH FOUNDATION

We are not starting from zero.

A master's thesis at Cinvestav Mérida evaluated Holothuria floridana juveniles in bottom cages for 104 days in a mangrove channel at Ría Lagartos. Its findings provide a local experimental basis for designing the next pilot.

03 / THE PILOT

What your support will make possible

The campaign is intended to fund the transition from experimental evidence to a documented pilot. Final scope depends on the published budget, permits, lawful juvenile availability and site conditions.

PHASE 01

Preparation and permits

Site characterization, regulatory review, biosecurity protocols and technical partnerships.

PHASE 02

Pilot infrastructure

Fabrication and deployment of cages, anchoring systems, protective materials and monitoring equipment.

PHASE 03

Controlled stocking

Sourcing or producing juveniles only through legal, authorized channels; acclimation, identification and batch records.

PHASE 04

Monitoring and learning

Tracking survival, growth, density, sediment, temperature, salinity, turbidity and associated fauna.

PHASE 05

Public reporting

Sharing methods, progress, results and lessons—including negative results and necessary changes.

PLANNING ALLOCATION

05 / FAQ & ACCOUNTABILITY

Transparency before promises

Trust starts by distinguishing research, a pilot and any future commercial operation.

Is the Kickstarter campaign live?

Not yet. This is the campaign presentation and preparation page. A pledge button will be enabled once the actual campaign has been approved and published on Kickstarter.

Which species is being considered?

The current technical project focuses on Holothuria floridana, studied in Ría Lagartos, Yucatán. Pilot animals will be selected and sourced only after scientific review, lawful-origin verification and applicable authorizations.

Will wild sea cucumbers be collected?

No unauthorized capture or collection is proposed. Broodstock or juveniles must be obtained through a legal, documented route consistent with conservation and fisheries rules.

Is commercial production or export guaranteed?

No. The campaign supports applied research and pilot validation, not a guaranteed commercial harvest. Production feasibility and any future sales depend on results, permits, animal health and traceability.

What if the site or method does not work?

Findings will be reported transparently. The project may adjust stocking density, structures, season or site, or stop if environmental or regulatory risks cannot be mitigated.

How will backers be recognized?

Final rewards and delivery estimates will appear on the official campaign. Digital rewards do not depend on harvest or biological success.
