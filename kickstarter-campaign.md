## Short description
Help MIPEZ build the foundations for responsible freshwater pearl cultivation in Yucatán, Mexico—where aquaculture research meets the beauty of nacre and a new vision for contemporary pearl fashion.

## The story
A pearl is not manufactured in a factory. It is formed over time, through a biological process that creates layers of nacre with distinctive depth, color and iridescence. That natural individuality is what makes pearls enduring objects of beauty—and what inspires MIPEZ.

We are developing a freshwater pearl project in Yucatán, Mexico. Our ambition is to connect responsible aquaculture research with a contemporary design language: Luxury Pearl Fashion and Contemporary Pearl Streetwear, exploring both Full Pearl Construction and Pearl Fusion. Future design directions include statement garments, tops, bags, wallets and wearable objects.

But before there can be a collection, there must be a responsible way to cultivate.

MIPEZ is currently at the research and pilot-development stage. The freshwater mussel species has not yet been selected. We must first identify a suitable native or legally authorized candidate, confirm its conservation and health status, validate its water and husbandry requirements, and secure all applicable authorizations. We are designing a contained, land-based pilot in Yucatán, with independent tanks, quarantine, recirculation, filtration, monitoring and effluent controls.

We are not presenting an established farm or promising an immediate harvest. We are inviting backers to help build the first chapter: the research, infrastructure and creative work needed to determine whether a responsible freshwater pearl project can succeed.

## Why this matters
- **Nature’s masterpieces:** pearls carry organic color, form and iridescence that cannot be standardized.
- **Cultural meaning:** pearls have long been associated with heritage, celebration, identity and meaningful gifts.
- **A Mexican perspective:** MIPEZ is exploring a project rooted in Yucatán and designed to speak to audiences in the United States and Canada.
- **Science before scale:** species selection, lawful sourcing, water quality, animal health and environmental safeguards come before commercial production.
- **A new design language:** our long-term creative direction brings pearl craftsmanship into both heirloom-inspired luxury and contemporary streetwear.

## What Kickstarter support will help us do
The final budget will be based on supplier quotations and technical review. Funding is intended to advance:
1. Species screening, specialist review and legal/regulatory assessment.
2. Site and water characterization in Yucatán.
3. A contained pilot system: tanks, plumbing, pumps, filtration, aeration, monitoring and backup systems.
4. Quarantine, biosecurity, water testing and effluent-management measures.
5. Husbandry trials to evaluate survival, feeding, condition and water stability.
6. Early design development and prototyping for future pearl-inspired fashion and objects.
7. Campaign fulfillment, packaging, cross-border shipping, platform/payment fees, taxes and a realistic contingency.

A final campaign budget must state exact amounts, supplier quotes, milestones and the portion reserved for fees and fulfillment. Do not publish a funding goal until those figures are verified.

## Project milestones
- **Stage 1 — Identify:** taxonomic screening, lawful sourcing, conservation and health review, site/water assessment.
- **Stage 2 — Build:** install and test the contained pilot without animals; verify hydraulics, filtration, alarms, emergency aeration and effluent procedures.
- **Stage 3 — Validate husbandry:** conduct appropriately reviewed trials with controls; monitor survival, feeding, condition and water quality.
- **Stage 4 — Pearl research:** only if permitted and supervised by a qualified specialist, assess an experimental pearl procedure and post-procedure recovery.
- **Stage 5 — Design:** develop product specifications and a collection only after actual harvest characteristics and quality are known.

## Risks and transparency
- **Species uncertainty:** no target freshwater mussel species has been selected. If no suitable and authorized species is identified, the project will not proceed with that species.
- **Permitting and sourcing:** acquisition, transport, culture, implantation, harvest and export may require different approvals. These must be checked with the relevant Mexican and destination-country authorities.
- **Biological timeline:** freshwater pearl culture is a multi-year process in published examples. A Japanese case documented approximately 3–4 years of culture; MIPEZ’s actual cycle is unknown and must not be represented as a guaranteed delivery date.
- **Variable results:** survival, pearl formation, shape, size, luster, color and marketable yield are not guaranteed.
- **Pilot performance:** pumps, filtration, water chemistry, feed availability, power reliability and biosecurity may require redesign.
- **Cross-border fulfillment:** final products, materials, country of origin, customs classification, duties, taxes, shipping costs and delivery destinations must be confirmed before physical rewards are offered.

We will communicate progress honestly, including delays, failed trials and changes in direction. Backers are supporting a creative and research project—not purchasing equity, receiving financial returns or buying a guaranteed share of future production.
